Frequently Asked Questions

Is SkillSwap free for students?

Yes, SkillSwap is 100% free! It is based entirely on peer-to-peer knowledge exchange.

Do I need to be an expert to offer a skill?

Not at all! As long as you can help a beginner understand a topic, subject, or practical tool, you can offer a skill swap.

How do skill swap sessions take place?

Students can meet up on campus, connect over Discord, Zoom, or organize study groups at a local library.

How do ratings and reviews work?

After completing a swap session, both students can leave constructive feedback and star ratings on each other's profile.
